Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. Enter your name and roll number to get a token.

What if I miss my turn?

Go to the counter and ask the staff. They can call you again or add you back to the queue.

Can I join more than one queue?

Yes, but only one token per counter.

Is my roll number visible to others?

No. The live board shows only the last four characters.
