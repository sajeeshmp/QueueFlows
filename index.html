Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. Enter your name and roll number to get a token.

What if I miss my turn?

Go to the counter and ask the staff. They can call you again or add you back at the end of the queue.

Can I join two queues at once?

Yes, one token per counter. You cannot take two tokens at the same counter.

How is the wait time calculated?

The number of people ahead of you plus one, multiplied by the average service time of that counter.

About QueueFlow

A mini project for the Web Development lab, built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. It shows how a campus can cut crowding at service counters.

  • CSS: selectors, box model, colours and responsive layout
  • JavaScript: arrays, objects, conditions, loops, functions and events
